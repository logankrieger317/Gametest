# 💎 Gem Rush — Idle Miner

A mindless-fun idle mining game in the style of *Idle Zombie Miner*. No fail state, no pressure — just watch the coins roll in and tap shiny things.

## How to play

- **Miners dig automatically.** Little bulldozers drive to the ore mounds, scoop up gems, and haul them to the **SELL** pad for coins. You literally don't have to do anything.
- **Tap the sparkling gems** scattered around the ore mounds to collect them instantly. Rapid taps build a combo multiplier.
- **Tap the treasure chest** when it appears for a big coin bonus.
- **Spend coins** on the three buttons at the bottom:
  - **⛏ MINER** — add another bulldozer
  - **⚡ SPEED** — miners move and dig faster
  - **💰 VALUE** — every gem is worth more
- **Unlock new ore zones** by tapping the locked gates when you can afford them: Crystal → Gold → Ruby → Prism, each worth way more than the last.
- **Come back later** — your miners keep working while you're away (up to 8 hours of offline earnings).

Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## Run it

No build, no dependencies — it's a single file.

- Open `index.html` in any browser, **or**
- Serve it locally: `python3 -m http.server` then visit http://localhost:8000

Works on desktop (click) and mobile (tap).
